# Taking Your Blood Pressure Pills

High blood pressure is common. Pills can help. Take your pill at the same time each day. Morning works well for most people.

## Simple steps

- Put your pills next to your toothbrush.
- Take one pill with a full glass of water.
- Check the label if you are not sure.
- Call your clinic if you feel dizzy.

## If you miss a pill

Do not take two pills at once. Skip the pill you missed. Take the next pill at the normal time. Write it down so you remember.

Ask your nurse or pharmacist if you have questions. No question is too small.
